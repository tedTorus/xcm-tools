Fixes #2674

### Description
`PapiApi.signAndSubmit` previously discarded the execution result (`result.ok` and `result.dispatchError`) and blindly returned the transaction hash. This caused on-chain failures (like `ExtrinsicFailed` due to insufficient funds) to be silently reported as successful submissions to the caller.

### Solution
Updated `signAndSubmit` to check `result.ok`. If the extrinsic fails, it now throws a `SubmitTransactionError` containing the `dispatchError`, perfectly aligning its behavior with its sibling method `signAndSubmitFinalized`.
