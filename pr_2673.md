Fixes #2673

### Description
In `buildOriginInfo`, `selectedCurrency.balanceAfter` was calculated simply as `balance - assetAmount`, completely ignoring the origin fee. When the fee is paid in the same asset being transferred, this resulted in an artificially inflated `balanceAfter`. Consequently, `sufficient` could evaluate to `true` even when the real remaining balance dipped below the existential deposit (ED), leading to failed transactions or account reaping.

### Solution
Updated `buildSelectedCurrency` to dynamically determine if the current asset is the fee asset (checking against `originFeeAsset` or resolving the native asset if `isOriginFeeAsset` is false). If it matches, the `originFee` is correctly subtracted from `balanceAfter`. This ensures the ED check (`sufficient`) is evaluated against the true remaining balance.
